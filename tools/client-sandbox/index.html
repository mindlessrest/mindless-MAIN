Mindless client sandbox

Click to capture the mouse. WASD move, Space jump, Shift sneak, left click to swing, Right Shift opens the click GUI, Esc releases the mouse.

The array list, the click GUI and the effects run the same geometry the client does, so what is tuned here is what ships.
